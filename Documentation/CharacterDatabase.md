# Character Database

## Rights policy
Crew leaders and the supporting roster are a **creative casting proposal** of fictionalized, alternate-universe versions
of real artists. No real person has approved participation. Biographies, hometowns and regional origins must be verified
before being presented as fact. Until rights are cleared, the game shows original placeholder names
(`CastingInfo.PlaceholderName`), placeholder models and placeholder voices, and no real recordings or lyrics are used.

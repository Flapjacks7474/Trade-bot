    How to play

    Left thumb: move (push far to sprint). Right thumb: look. Yellow button: talk, use, drive.
    Fights: JAB, JAB, HEAVY is a combo. Tap BLOCK right before a hit to parry. GRAB then HEAVY throws. Fill the gold meter for a FINISH.
    WASD move · Shift sprint · drag the mouse to look · E talk / use / drive · Esc menu · 1–9 dialogue.
    Fights: J jab · K heavy · I kick · L grab · Space block (tap = parry) · Shift dodge · O counter · U finisher · T taunt.
    Driving: W/S gas/brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake · Shift nitrous · C camera · E get out / park / start race.
